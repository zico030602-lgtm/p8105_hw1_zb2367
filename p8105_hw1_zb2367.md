## Problem 1

``` r
data("penguins", package = "palmerpenguins")
```

The `penguins` dataset has 344 rows (one penguin per row) and 8 columns.
It covers Antarctic penguins of three species: Adelie, Chinstrap,
Gentoo, and the islands are Biscoe, Dream, Torgersen. Measurements
include bill length and depth (mm), flipper length (mm), and body mass
(g). It also records sex (female or male, with missing values) and year
(2007–2009).

The mean flipper length is 200.92 mm. This excludes 2 missing values.


The numeric mean is 0.064. The logical mean is 0.5, the proportion above
zero. Character and factor means return `NA` with warnings because these
types are not numeric or logical.

``` r
as.numeric(sample_data$positive)
as.numeric(sample_data$character_value)
as.numeric(sample_data$factor_value)
```

Logical values become 1 (`TRUE`) or 0 (`FALSE`), explaining their
proportion mean. Letters become `NA` with a warning because they are not
numeric strings. Factors become level codes (`low` = 1, `medium` = 2,
`high` = 3). These codes label categories, so their mean is not a
meaningful category average; `mean()` on the original factor returns
`NA`.
